Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026. Applies to Plain FX and Day For It for iOS, published by Will Deasy / Electric Future.

Summary

We build small utilities. We don’t sell your personal data. We don’t run ads in these apps. Where location is used, it’s for a feature you choose, not for advertising profiles.

Plain FX

  • Exchange rates — fetched from a third-party mid-market rate service and cached on your device so conversion can work offline.
  • Location (optional) — if you allow it, may be used to set a local currency as your base. Not used on launch unless you enable that behaviour. Not sold.
  • On-device preferences — currency board order and similar settings stay on your phone.
  • No ads — Plain FX does not show third-party advertising.

Day For It

  • Location (when you ask) — used for features like ranking nearby beaches or check-in while you request them. Not continuous background tracking for ads.
  • Environmental / transit data — the app requests public or licensed weather, water-quality, and related feeds to show swim/surf/crowd/Comfort guidance. Those requests go to the relevant providers; treat displayed conditions as indicative only.
  • Hearts, check-ins, badges — stored on your device for your own progress; not a public leaderboard.
  • No ads — Day For It does not show third-party advertising.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information.
  • We don’t use your data to build advertising profiles for third parties.
  • We don’t require an account to use the core features of these apps.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13. Don’t provide personal information about children through them.

Changes

We may update this page when the apps change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
