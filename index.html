Plain FX

Simple currency conversion across the currencies on your board. Type any amount and the others update from the same shared quote. Mid-market rates refresh about once an hour. Not a bank rate and not an offer to trade.

Common questions

  • Rates look different from my bank — expected; Plain FX shows mid-market quotes, not retail FX.
  • Offline — the last quote stays on your phone so you can still convert.
  • Location — optional; when enabled it can set your local currency as the base. The app does not use GPS on launch.

Day For It

Sydney beach picks for today — swim, surf, crowd, and Comfort — so you can choose a spot without juggling forecast apps. Information is indicative only; always check local signage, patrol flags, and conditions before you swim.

Common questions

  • Location — used only when you ask (for example Near me / check-in), not continuously in the background for marketing.
  • Water quality — Swim draws on Beachwatch-style forecasts; treat as guidance, not a guarantee.
  • Hearts vs check-ins — hearts are want-to-go; the log is where you’ve been.
